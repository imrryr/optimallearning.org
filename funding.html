Current and past funded projects.

Current support

NSF | 2023–2027

Improving STEM K-12 learning using optimal spaced retrieval in Podsie

Co-PI Philip Pavlik; PI Paulo Carvalho at Carnegie Mellon University. Memphis award amount: $226,060. Memory models guide spaced practice in an existing adaptive platform.

Completed support

IES | 2019–2023, including a no-cost extension

Using Adaptive Practice to Improve Recall and Understanding in Postsecondary Anatomy and Physiology

PI Philip Pavlik. Total award: $1,240,151 (R305A190448). Adaptive practice and dialogue for recall and understanding in postsecondary anatomy and physiology. The original award period ended June 30, 2022; a no-cost extension continued the project through 2023.

NSF | 2020–2023

The Learner Data Institute: Harnessing the Data Revolution to Make the Learning Ecosystem More Effective, Efficient, and Engaging

Co-PI Philip Pavlik; PI Vasile Rus. Total award reported in the CV: $2,584,309 (1934745). Support for developing the Learner Data Institute. The dates describe Pavlik’s project participation as recorded in the CV.

Schmidt Family Foundation | 2018–2019

Memphis and McGraw Hill Education postdoctoral work

PI Philip Pavlik. Support reported in the CV: $200,000, through a Carnegie Mellon University subaward (A18-0103).

Office of Naval Research | 2016–2018

Integration of Intelligent Tutoring Systems for Electronics

Co-investigator Philip Pavlik; PI Arthur Graesser. Total award: $1,491,371 (N00014-16-C3027). Conversational tutoring for electronics.

NSF | 2015–2019

CIF21 DIBBs: Building a Scalable Infrastructure for Data-Driven Discovery and Innovation in Education

PI Philip Pavlik for the Memphis work. Memphis subaward from Carnegie Mellon University: $750,000 (ACI1443068); this is not the total collaborative award. Collaboration on LearnSphere educational data analysis.

Army Research Lab | 2012–2017

Generalized Intelligent Framework for Tutors

Co-investigator Philip Pavlik; PI Xiangen Hu. Total award: $1,289,545 (W911NF-12-2-0030). Work on the GIFT book series.

Pittsburgh Science of Learning Center | 2009–2011

Motivational effects in vocabulary learning: difficulty and strategy use

PI Philip Pavlik. Project support: $70,000. Experiments on the interaction of difficulty and strategy use.

IES | 2007–2012

Bridging the Bridge to Algebra: Measuring and Optimizing the Influence of Prerequisite Skills on a Pre-Algebra Curriculum

PI Philip Pavlik. Total award: $1,120,955 (R305B070487). Prerequisite practice for the Algebra Cognitive Tutor.

Private support | 2005–2008

Models of learning to optimize instruction

PI Philip Pavlik. Support from Ronald Zdrojkowski: $411,000, for postdoctoral work with Kenneth Koedinger applying theoretical learning models to instruction.

Project roles and funding history are documented in Philip Pavlik’s CV (PDF). Award totals and Memphis subawards are labeled separately above.
