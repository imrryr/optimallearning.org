NSF Grant Detail

Scalable Infrastructure for Data-Driven Discovery and Innovation in Education

This project supported infrastructure for sharing, analyzing, and collaborating around educational data. It connects directly to the learning analytics side of the lab's work and to resources such as LearnSphere and LKT.

Summary

Project summary

The project contributed to LearnSphere, a community infrastructure for sharing educational datasets, analyses, and workflows. Its larger aim was to help learning researchers, course developers, and instructors make better use of educational data in both research and course improvement.

Completed support ran from 2015 through 2019. Philip Pavlik led the Memphis work as principal investigator of a $750,000 Memphis subaward from Carnegie Mellon University; this amount is not the total collaborative award.
