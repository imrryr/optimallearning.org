Director

Philip I. Pavlik Jr., Full Professor and Dunavant Professor

Director of the Optimal Learning Lab

Philip Pavlik has been Full Professor and Dunavant Professor at the University of Memphis since September 2025 and Chief Editor of the Journal of Educational Data Mining since 2024. His research connects computational theories of learning, memory, and transfer with interpretable learner models and adaptive educational software, including MoFaCTS, LKT, and the emerging TutorScript/SPARC work.

He earned his PhD in cognitive psychology at Carnegie Mellon University in 2005, advised by John Anderson, and completed postdoctoral work with Kenneth Koedinger in Carnegie Mellon's Human-Computer Interaction Institute.
