IES Grant Detail

Adaptive Practice to Improve Recall and Understanding in Postsecondary Anatomy and Physiology

IES-supported research on whether adaptive practice can improve recall and understanding in postsecondary anatomy and physiology.

Summary

Project summary

This IES-supported project studied whether adaptive practice could improve recall and understanding in postsecondary anatomy and physiology. It belongs to the lab's broader effort to move from formal learning models toward instructional systems that can make better decisions about when and what students should practice.

On the current site, this project is best understood as an applied grant that connects the lab's work on memory, scheduling, and learner modeling to a high-content higher-education setting.
