Cognitive Science and Learning Technology

Designing practice that helps people learn more, retain more, and transfer more.

The Optimal Learning Lab studies how memory models, knowledge tracing, and adaptive educational systems can be turned into practical tools for instruction. Our work connects theory, data, and software design so that learning environments can make better decisions about timing, sequence, and support.

New research innovation

TutorScript: production rules for intelligent tutoring

SPARC combines learner models, explicit instructional rules, and generative AI to study adaptive tutoring. TutorScript is the language used to express those rules: it makes the choice of instructional action available for inspection, while AI generates the selected explanation, example, figure, or question. These emerging research capabilities are being tested in controlled deployments.

What We Do

Research that moves from theory to usable systems.

Model Learning Over Time

We build and evaluate formal models of learning and memory that capture spacing, recency, practice history, and changing performance.

Design Adaptive Practice

We translate those models into educational software that schedules practice, prioritizes items, and adapts to the learner's history.

Study Complex Knowledge

We investigate learning beyond isolated facts, including connected concepts, dependencies across ideas, and instructional structure.

Why this matters

Better learning systems should not just deliver content. They should make defensible decisions about when, what, and how practice should happen.

That idea sits at the center of the lab's work across memory, modeling, and instructional software.
